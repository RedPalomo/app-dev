# SAW Movie Series


> *"I want to play a game."*

---

## Overview

The **Saw** franchise is a horror saga centered around John Kramer, also known as **Jigsaw**, a morally complex killer who captures his victims and puts them through deadly "games" to test their will to live. It has become one of the most iconic horror franchises of the 21st century.

- **Genre:** Horror, Psychological Thriller
- **Created by:** James Wan & Leigh Whannell
- **First Released:** October 29, 2004
- **Total Films:** 10 (as of 2023)
- **Notable Characters:**
  - John Kramer / Jigsaw
  - Amanda Young
  - Detective Hoffman
  - Dr. Lawrence Gordon

---

## Top 3 Saw Traps

1. **Reverse Bear Trap** – A horrifying device that will rip the jaw open unless disarmed in time.
2. **Needle Pit** – A pit filled with dirty syringes, used as a punishment.
3. **Shotgun Carousel** – A rotating trap where only one out of six can survive.
